What the board does with those signals

Before powering up

Don't power the servo from 3V3The connector power pins are 3.3 V. The servo and the HC-SR04 need 5 V, and the servo's current spikes can reset the board.
Keep the divider on ECHOWithout R1/R2 the sensor puts 5 V on IO32. A 3.3 V sensor (HC-SR04P, RCWL-1601) can skip the divider and run from 3V3.
Servo pin differs between sketchesNazr and Radar use IO21; the Web Lab bridge firmware uses IO22. Change PIN_SERVO so they match your wiring.
